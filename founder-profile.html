about the founder

Arvind Kumar Dwivedi Vastu Interior & Décor Consultant

With over ten years of experience as an independent Interior Consultant, Mr. Dwivedi has developed a practical, client-centric approach focused on creating personalised solutions that reflect each client's needs, lifestyle and aspirations.

Before entering the field of interior consulting, he built extensive experience in the service industry, serving with dedication in customer-facing roles at leading organisations, including Kotak Mahindra Life Insurance Co. Ltd., ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Co. Ltd. and Max New York Life Insurance Co. Ltd. This professional journey gave him valuable insight into market dynamics, customer expectations and relationship management.

His strong communication, motivational and leadership abilities have enabled him to build lasting client relationships and encourage effective team collaboration. His commitment to dependable after-sales service continues to be one of the defining strengths of his work.

Academic & Professional Qualifications

Mr. Dwivedi's academic journey began at Central School, Bangalore, and continued across several cities in India. He completed his bachelor's degree from Kanpur University. His professional education began in 2001 at JIM, where he completed his PGDIRM qualification, followed by a specialisation in Marketing from IIM.

Philosophy: Vastu & Positive Energy

Mr. Dwivedi believes that thoughtfully designed surroundings can positively influence the way people live, work and feel. In his approach, Vastu principles, colour selection, spatial planning and functional design work together to create environments that encourage balance, comfort and positive energy.

Personal Journey & Resilience

From surviving tetanus in infancy to overcoming stage-three throat cancer later in life, he has faced extraordinary medical challenges with courage and discipline. These experiences gave him a deeper appreciation for positive surroundings, balanced living and thoughtfully designed spaces.

his story

Mr. Dwivedi's journey is defined by creativity, resilience and a determination to find better ways of living. After beginning his entrepreneurial journey in real estate, he discovered his true calling in Vastu-based interior design and décor. A former district-level volleyball player, Mr. Dwivedi continues to approach life and work with strength, focus and optimism.

the vastu way of life

Mr. Dwivedi often draws inspiration from the legendary city of Indraprastha—the capital of the Pandavas—which, according to tradition, was designed with divine architectural wisdom. He believes that a thoughtfully planned, Vastu-aligned home can offer its occupants a deeper sense of comfort, satisfaction and harmony.
