Beyond structural design, we consult on how a space feels once it's built — the styling, textures, and details that bring it to life. Good decor doesn't fight the architecture around it; it complements it.

From colour palettes to lighting and final styling, our decor consultancy picks up where design planning leaves off, so the finished space feels considered rather than assembled piece by piece.

  • Color palette & theme selection
  • Furnishing, upholstery & soft furnishing recommendations
  • Lighting design consultancy (ambient, task & accent)
  • Wall treatments, textures & decorative elements
  • Accessorizing & final styling guidance
  • Seasonal or festive decor consultancy on request
