Residential Architectural Planning

For this new-construction project, we were brought in early to review the proposed floor plans for structural feasibility and building orientation before the design was finalised. Catching issues at this stage meant the family avoided costly reworking later in construction.

We coordinated between the family's architect and contractor throughout, making sure the structural plan, the design intent and the Vastu direction they'd asked for were all working from the same set of decisions.

  • Floor plan structural feasibility review
  • Building orientation and site evaluation
  • Coordination between architect and contractor
  • Vastu-aware structural guidance
