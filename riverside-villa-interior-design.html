Riverside Villa Interior Design

This riverside villa came to us after two separate designers had already worked on parts of the home, leaving the layout and finishes pulling in different directions. Our brief was to bring the whole interior — living areas, bedrooms and the outdoor-facing spaces — under one coherent design direction.

We started with a full space-planning review, reworking furniture zones and circulation so the living and dining areas made better use of the river-facing windows. From there, material and colour palettes were finalised room by room, with an emphasis on finishes that would hold up to the humidity near the water.

  • Whole-home space planning review
  • Room-by-room material and colour palette
  • Furniture layout and lighting plan
  • Coordination with the client's existing contractor
