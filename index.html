how we work

From consultation to coordinated advice

Our structured methodology ensures consistent service delivery across all project types — from residential interiors to commercial spaces, architectural planning to Vastu consultancy — maintaining quality benchmarks at every engagement level.

01. consultation

Initial discovery session to document project scope, spatial requirements, design preferences, budget parameters, and timeline expectations through detailed site analysis or remote consultation.

02. assessment

Comprehensive evaluation integrating interior design feasibility, architectural constraints, structural considerations, and Vastu compliance — delivered as unified analysis, not fragmented reports.

03. recommendation

Coordinated design solutions with itemized implementation roadmap, material specifications, vendor references, cost estimates, and phased execution timelines — all pre-validated against project constraints.

04. ongoing advisory

Optional execution support including vendor liaison, site supervision, quality checks, design clarifications, and post-installation review — ensuring recommendations translate accurately from documentation to reality.
