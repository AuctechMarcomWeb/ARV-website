how we work

From consultation to coordinated advice

A consistent four-step process guides every consultation, whether it's a design review, a full project plan, or a Vastu assessment.

01. consultation

Understand your space, requirement, and any Vastu concerns — over a call or a site visit.

02. assessment

Site or layout review, covering design, structural, and Vastu factors together — not in isolation.

03. recommendation

A single, coordinated set of recommendations, not siloed advice from separate specialists.

04. ongoing advisory

Support through execution if you need it — from planning through to final handover.
