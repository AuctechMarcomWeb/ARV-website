about us

One consultancy for design, structure and direction

ARV VASTU INTERIORS Consultants is a multidisciplinary design consultancy delivering integrated solutions across interior design, architecture, project planning, and Vastu consultancy. With over a decade of experience in the built environment sector, we bring a coordinated approach that aligns aesthetics, functionality, and spatial harmony under one advisory framework.

Our practice philosophy is rooted in practical, client-centric design thinking. We specialise in translating complex spatial requirements into actionable, budget-conscious design strategies that respect both modern design principles and traditional Vastu guidelines — without compromise on either front.

What sets us apart is our single-point accountability model. Unlike fragmented consultancies where architectural, interior, and Vastu advice comes from separate specialists — often conflicting — we provide unified recommendations reviewed holistically before they reach you. This eliminates redundancy, reduces project timelines, and ensures design coherence across all touchpoints.

how we work

From consultation to coordinated advice

Our structured methodology ensures consistent service delivery across all project types — from residential interiors to commercial spaces, architectural planning to Vastu consultancy — maintaining quality benchmarks at every engagement level.

01. consultation

Initial discovery session to document project scope, spatial requirements, design preferences, budget parameters, and timeline expectations through detailed site analysis or remote consultation.

02. assessment

Comprehensive evaluation integrating interior design feasibility, architectural constraints, structural considerations, and Vastu compliance — delivered as unified analysis, not fragmented reports.

03. recommendation

Coordinated design solutions with itemized implementation roadmap, material specifications, vendor references, cost estimates, and phased execution timelines — all pre-validated against project constraints.

04. ongoing advisory

Optional execution support including vendor liaison, site supervision, quality checks, design clarifications, and post-installation review — ensuring recommendations translate accurately from documentation to reality.
